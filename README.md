## About the app

Salesforce Revenue Execution is a read-only Salesforce app that shows leaders where pipeline needs corrective action:

- See open pipeline, past-due exposure, and closed outcomes in an executive-ready overview.
- Isolate the deals that need corrective action, each with its owner, days late, and the next step recorded in Salesforce.
- Review won and lost outcomes, booked value, and the lost deals behind each product.
- See where late or lost deals sit across product families, product lines, and individual products.
- Move from executive summaries to the supporting Salesforce records each user is authorized to access.

Every view respects each user's Salesforce access, and the app never edits a record. Pipeline and booked value stay separate from recognized revenue.
