# Revenue Execution privacy notice

## In short

Revenue Execution runs entirely inside your Salesforce org. It reads your data as each viewing user and sends none of it to us or anyone else.

## What Revenue Execution does in your org

- Reads Opportunities, Accounts, users, products, and related setup as the viewing user, with that user's sharing and field access.
- Writes only its own settings, when an administrator you allow saves them. If you turn on Deal Alerts, it also keeps a send log in your org and sends email through Salesforce to your active users.
- Makes no calls to outside services, uses no tracking, and never contacts us.

The full privacy notice comes with general availability. Questions: [support@opsgs.com](mailto:support@opsgs.com).
