## About the app

Salesforce Revenue Execution is a read-only Salesforce app that helps revenue teams:

- See open pipeline, past-due exposure, and booked outcomes in an executive-ready overview.
- Focus sellers and managers on deals that need attention, with clear timing, action signals, and ownership.
- Review won and lost performance, win rate, booked value, and sales-cycle trends.
- Understand product performance across product families, product lines, and individual products.
- Move from executive summaries to the supporting Salesforce records each user is authorized to access.

The app keeps pipeline and booked value clearly separated from recognized revenue so leaders can use each metric in the right business context.
