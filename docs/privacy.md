# Revenue Execution privacy notice

This page explains how Revenue Execution handles your data.

## In short

Revenue Execution runs entirely inside your Salesforce org. It reads your data as each viewing user and sends none of it to us or anyone else.

## What Revenue Execution does in your org

- It reads Opportunities, Accounts, users, products, and related setup, as the viewing user, with that user's sharing and field access.
- It writes only its own settings, when an administrator you allow saves them. If you turn on Deal Alerts, it also keeps a send log in your org and sends emails through Salesforce to your active users.
- It makes no calls to outside services, uses no tracking, and does not contact us.

The full privacy notice comes with general availability. Questions: [support@opsgs.com](mailto:support@opsgs.com).
