# Revenue Execution release notes

Find your installed version in Setup > Apps > Packaging > Installed Packages. Before any upgrade, turn Deal Alerts off ([Admin guide: Deal Alerts](admin-guide.md#deal-alerts)), then turn it on again afterwards.

## First release (upcoming)

Not yet released. The first version will include:

- Five read-only dashboards, each with My results, Team, Leadership, and Executive views, period presets, and a person picker ([User guide](user-guide.md)):
  - Revenue Overview.
  - Pipeline Schedule, with Escalated, At risk, and On track sections, and search.
  - Sales Outcomes, with Close reasons: lost and won deals by the reasons your team recorded.
  - Product Overview, with each product's lost deals and recorded reasons.
  - Team Performance: your reporting line by won value, never ranked.
- Exact totals for teams closing up to 25,000 deals a year. On large pipelines, views that include open deals work best by team or region ([Admin guide](admin-guide.md#before-you-install)).
- Admin / Setup ([Admin guide](admin-guide.md)). **Get started** gets you ready in four steps. **Configure Fields** lets you choose your own deal value, close date, owner credit, next step, segment, and product fields, preview the effect, and save in the page. **Health** runs every access and setting check, with the fix on each row.
- Your own field labels and picklist values, calendar or fiscal quarters, and your deal-size bands.
- With multiple currencies, amounts are withheld and counts stay exact. Small samples show counts, not rates.
- Deal Alerts, off by default: past-due and missing-next-step emails with manager escalation, and monthly product and win-rate summaries, previewed before anything sends.
- Every query runs as the viewing user, with their sharing and field access. The only writes are Revenue Execution's own settings and, once Deal Alerts is on, its send log and emails.
