# Revenue Execution support

Email **[support@opsgs.com](mailto:support@opsgs.com)**.

Most setup questions are answered on the page: open Admin / Setup > **Health**, choose **All**, and follow the **To fix** steps on each row. The [Admin guide](admin-guide.md#troubleshooting) explains every row.

## What to include

1. The Revenue Execution version from Setup > Apps > Packaging > Installed Packages.
2. Your Salesforce edition, and whether multiple currencies are enabled.
3. The area, status, and title of each Fail or Warning row in Health, and the steps you already tried.
4. For a dashboard question, the dashboard, the view (My results, Team, Leadership, or Executive), and the period.

Never send passwords, usernames, record IDs, screenshots of customer data, or exported records.

## Security issues

To report a suspected security issue, email support@opsgs.com with "Security" in the subject. Please don't post it publicly.
